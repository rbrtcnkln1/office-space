## Review notes

- Kept Codex's lines as written. The jokes are original and generic: no quotes, names, catchphrases or brands from real films or shows. "In a world of..." is the trailer trope, not a quotation.
- "None of the context overhead" is accurate: the mod only draws UI and adds nothing to Claude's context.
- Captions are 8 words or fewer so they fit an 800 px or 1280 px frame at a readable size.
- Open question for the final cut: a real voiceover (human or synthetic) and a music bed. Both are out of scope for this rough cut.
